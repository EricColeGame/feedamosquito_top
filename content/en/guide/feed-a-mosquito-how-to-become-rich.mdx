## The Money Loop in Feed A Mosquito, Explained Simply

The trap is that the loop looks linear when it really is not. Basic feeding gives you a small, steady trickle of coins, while multipliers and rebirth bonuses give you exponential jumps. Players who only feed stay poor for hours. Players who feed *and* reinvest climb steadily.

| Income layer | What it is | How it pays | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
